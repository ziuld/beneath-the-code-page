# Brand application and logo clarification

Source: user-supplied brand master document and the user's explicit logo clarification. The original document and supplied PNG are preserved under `docs/reference/`.

The **B is the logo**, not merely a visual mood reference. Approved variants:

- Standalone B mark.
- B with the wireframe/contour background from the supplied image.

The available asset is the complete raster composition. A clean standalone/vector master has not been supplied. Do not claim that asset exists, redraw the B using an arbitrary font, or remove its background without a separate asset task. Small-size legibility, clear space and favicon treatment are design-system acceptance work.

Use the composition for suitable brand/learning placements. Keep editor surfaces calm and functional. Do not put a busy logo background behind code or essential text.

## Visual baseline

Canvas `#050505`; surface `#0B0B0B`; raised surface `#111111`; primary text `#EDEAE4`; secondary text `#92908B`; muted text `#66645F`; border `#292927`; strong border `#45433F`; bright text `#F7F7F5`.

Use display serif for editorial statements, sans-serif for UI and monospace for code/metadata. Exact font families and their redistribution licences remain open. Self-host selected fonts. Muted palette values must pass the contrast requirement for their actual role; decorative colours are not automatically suitable for essential labels or focus indicators.

Centralise colour, typography, spacing, widths, breakpoints, borders, radii, focus, motion and semantic states. Do not build independent page-level button/dialog/editor systems.

## Reusable components

Buttons, links, inputs, selects, alerts, dialogs, toasts, cards, tool/course cards, editor wrapper, tool workspace, navbar, footer, breadcrumbs, language selector, tabs, badges and lesson navigation. Build only what the next slice needs.

## Voice and education

Understanding over memorisation; clarity over spectacle. Use calm, precise explanations and systems mental models. Video-production rules in the master document guide educational tone; they do not require application video production, cloud AI services or a CMS.

## Acceptance

Keyboard navigation, visible/unobscured focus, 200% zoom, narrow-screen reflow, reduced motion, WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, accessible dialog focus management and no colour-only status. Check editor escape/Tab behaviour and screen-reader announcements manually as well as with automated tools.
